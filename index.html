Match tracking and analytics for competitive Valorant teams.

This is a private team tool. It records competitive Valorant matches round by round, then turns that history into the numbers a team actually uses to prepare: how each map is trending, which agent compositions are working, how individual players perform in specific roles, and what an upcoming opponent tends to do.

Accounts are invite-only and every roster's data is visible only to the people on it, so there is no public match database to browse here. The description below covers what the tool does for the teams that use it.

What it does

  • Round-by-round match records — log every round of a series, including the buy phase and economy on both sides.
  • Team, map, and agent analytics — win rates, attack/defense splits, and composition performance broken out by map.
  • Positioning heatmaps — visualize where kills and deaths happen across each map.
  • Player breakdowns — per-player stats filtered by map, agent, role, and date range.
  • Map veto planning — build and review pick/ban sequences against a specific opponent.
  • VOD review and scouting — attach match videos and review notes, and keep scouting reports next to an opponent's tendencies.

Getting access

Access is granted by invitation from a roster owner. If you are on a team that uses this tool, ask your roster owner for an invite link. If you already have an account, sign in with Google.
