Generator's University Team Tracking System

About

The Generator's University Team Tracking System is a private match-tracking and analytics tool for competitive Valorant teams. Teams record their matches round by round, and the site turns that history into map, agent, and player analytics.

Accounts are invite-only. Each roster's data is visible only to the people invited to it, so there is no public match database here.
