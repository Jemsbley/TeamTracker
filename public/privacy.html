Generator's University Team Tracking System

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Last updated 15 September 2026

This policy describes what the Generator's University Team Tracking System (“the site”), at tracker.jemsbley.dev, collects, why, and what is done with it. The site is a private, invite-only tool for competitive Valorant teams.

The short version. The site stores your email address, a display name you choose, and the match and roster data you enter. It shows that data to the people you share a roster with. It runs no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

1. Information collected

Account information from Google Sign-In

Signing in uses Google Sign-In. The site never receives, sees, or stores your Google password. From the verified sign-in token Google returns, the site stores:

  • your email address, which identifies your account;
  • your Google account identifier (the token's sub claim), recorded so the same Google account maps back to the same site account.

Your Google profile name and profile picture are not stored. Sign-in is refused if Google reports the email address as unverified.

Account information you provide

  • a display name you choose when you first sign in, shown to other members of rosters you belong to;
  • an account type (standard, administrator, or test) assigned by the site, which controls what you can access.

Content you enter

The substance of the site is data you and your teammates enter. That includes rosters and their members, player names and in-game aliases, staff profiles, series and individual maps, round-by-round match records, player statistics, map pick and ban sequences, links to match videos, written VOD review notes, and scouting reports on opponents.

Match data can also be entered by pasting exported match JSON from tracker.gg into the import tool. That paste happens entirely in your browser and the site does not contact tracker.gg or any other game data provider on your behalf; only the resulting match record is saved.

Technical information

The site does not run analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, session recording, or fingerprinting, and it sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The hosting providers named below keep their own standard server request logs, which ordinarily include IP addresses, timestamps, and requested URLs, and which are used for operating and securing the service.

2. Cookies and browser storage

After you sign in, the site stores a sign-in token in your browser's localStorage under the key team-tracker-token. It exists to keep you signed in and is the only thing the site itself stores in your browser. It is not a cookie and is not sent to any third party. Signing out removes it, as does clearing your browser's site data.

The Google Sign-In button is loaded from Google and may set its own cookies on Google's domains, governed by Google's Privacy Policy rather than this one.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to run the site, specifically to:

  • authenticate you and keep you signed in;
  • store your team's match data and show it back to you as analytics;
  • show your display name to other members of rosters you belong to, so they can see who recorded or edited what;
  • send and validate roster invitations;
  • diagnose errors and keep the service working.

Your information is not used for advertising or marketing, and it is not used to train machine-learning models.

4. Who your data is shared with

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for anyone else's commercial purposes. It is visible to:

  • Members of rosters you share. A roster's data is visible to the accounts invited to it, at owner, editor, or viewer level. Anyone on a roster can see the match data on that roster and the display names of its other members.
  • Site administrators. An administrator account can access account records and stored data for support and moderation.
  • Infrastructure providers that host the site on its behalf, listed in the next section.

Data may also be disclosed if legally required, for example in response to a valid legal request.

5. Where data is stored

The site is operated using third-party infrastructure providers who process data only to host the service:

  • Vercel — serves the website itself.
  • Railway — runs the API and hosts the PostgreSQL database where all account and match data is stored.
  • Google — provides sign-in.

These providers operate servers in the United States.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and match data is kept while the account is active, because the point of the tool is a historical record across seasons.

You can request deletion of your account at any time by emailing contact@jemsbley.dev from the address on the account. Deleting an account also deletes the rosters that account created, along with the players, matches, and scouting reports on them. If you created a roster that your teammates are still using, say so in your request and roster ownership can be transferred instead.

7. Your choices

  • Access and correction — most of your data is editable directly in the app; anything else can be requested by email.
  • Deletion — request it as described above.
  • Export — a copy of your data can be requested by email.
  • Revoking Google access — you can disconnect the site from your Google account in your Google account's security settings, which prevents future sign-ins.

8. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS, sign-in tokens expire, and access to roster data is checked on every request against your roster membership. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not store anything here that would be genuinely damaging if disclosed.

9. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email the address below and it will be removed.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. Material changes affecting how your data is used will also be communicated to account holders by email.

11. Contact

Questions, data requests, or privacy concerns: contact@jemsbley.dev.
