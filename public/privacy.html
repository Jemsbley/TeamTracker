Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

What we collect

Your email address and Google account identifier when you sign in with Google, a display name you choose, and the roster, match, and scouting data you enter.

How it is used

To sign you in, to store your team's data, and to show that data to you and the other members of rosters you belong to. There is no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking on this site.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, apart from the members of rosters you share and the hosting providers that run the site. Your data is not sold.

Deleting your data
